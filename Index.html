A bit of history of

Apple Inc. is a global technology leader that revolutionizes how the world connects, creates, and communicates. Founded in 1976 by Steve Jobs and Steve Wozniak, the Cupertino-based company has grown from a pioneering garage startup into one of the world's most valuable and influential brands. The company's trajectory was fundamentally shaped by the visionary leadership of Steve Jobs, whose relentless pursuit of perfection and revolutionary marketing redefined the tech landscape. His philosophy that "design is not just what it looks like and feels like, but how it works" remains embedded in the company's DNA today. Driven by this passion for innovation and elegant design, Apple seamlessly integrates premium hardware, intuitive software, and secure services. From the iconic iPhone, Mac, and iPad to cutting-edge wearables like the Apple Watch and Apple Vision Pro, Apple continues to honor Jobs' legacy by redefining user experience and shaping the future of consumer technology.
